Gymly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Gymly (the openGym application) is a workout and body-weight tracker. This policy explains what information the app processes and the choices available to you.

Information you choose to provide

  • Workout and body-weight records. These may include exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, dates, notes and body-weight entries. In the web app, app data is stored in your browser. In supported mobile builds, app data is stored on your device.
  • Google account information. If you choose Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication receives the Google account identity needed to sign you in. Gymly uses your account identifier, name, email address and profile image to represent your signed-in profile.
  • Health data. If you connect Google Health, Gymly requests write-only permission to send completed workout sessions and body-weight records to your Google Health account. The app does not read health history from Google Health. If you connect Health Connect or Apple Health, the operating system requests your permission for the particular health record types used by the app. Health Connect and HealthKit data is accessed on your device and is not sent to Gymly's server by that integration.

How information is used

Gymly uses this information to provide sign-in, save and display your training history, calculate training statistics, and perform a health-data sync only when you connect the relevant service and initiate or enable that feature. Google Health access tokens are kept in memory by the app and are not written to browser local storage.

Storage and service providers

Gymly uses Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and Firebase Analytics for app usage measurement. These services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. When you use Google Health, the app sends the selected workouts and body-weight records directly to Google's Health API using the write permissions you grant. Health Connect and Apple Health permissions and records are managed by Android and Apple respectively.

For users who sign in to a server-backed Gymly instance, account and app data may be stored by that instance's operator. The public Gymly sites are operated as a self-hosted deployment; access to server-stored data is limited by the deployed service and its operator. Guest-mode data remains in the browser or on the device.

Sharing and advertising

Gymly does not sell personal information or health data and does not use health data for advertising. Information is sent to Google only as needed for Google sign-in, Firebase Analytics, or a Google Health sync that you choose to use. The app does not send Health Connect or HealthKit records to Gymly's server.

Retention and your choices

Browser/device records remain there until you clear the app's data or remove them. You can disconnect a health service or revoke its permissions in the service or operating-system settings. You can sign out of Google in Gymly and manage the app's access from your Google Account security settings. Data already written to Google Health or another health service is governed by that provider; remove it there if you want it deleted.

For account or server-data questions, contact allinpokerr011@gmail.com and identify the account and site you used. We will use that information to locate and respond to your request.

Security and children

Gymly uses the security controls provided by the browser, mobile operating system, Firebase and the deployed server. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Gymly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly ask children under 13 to provide personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published. Questions: allinpokerr011@gmail.com.
